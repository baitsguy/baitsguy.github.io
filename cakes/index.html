Chocolate peanut butter cake

Two 8-inch layers. Batter and frosting scaled to ⅔; ganache unchanged for top coverage.

Adapted from Preppy Kitchen’s recipe. Flat-layer guide.

Cake

  • 160ml buttermilk
  • 2 large eggs
  • 120ml vegetable oil
  • 2 tsp vanilla
  • 67g unsweetened cocoa
  • 220g all-purpose flour
  • 367g sugar
  • 1⅓ tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 160ml hot coffee or water

Frosting

  • 227g softened unsalted butter
  • 217g creamy, no-stir peanut butter
  • 2 tsp vanilla
  • ⅙ tsp salt
  • 400g powdered sugar
  • 40–50ml milk

Ganache

  • 113g chopped dark chocolate
  • 80ml heavy cream

Method

  1. Grease two 8-inch pans; line bottoms with parchment. Heat oven to 350°F.
  2. For flatter layers, soak fabric cake strips 5–10 minutes, squeeze, and fit around pans.
  3. Whisk dry cake ingredients. Combine buttermilk, eggs, oil, vanilla; stir into dry mixture, then add hot coffee.
  4. Divide evenly. Bake 30–35 minutes, until centers spring back; strips may extend baking. Cool 15 minutes; unmold and cool completely.
  5. Beat butter and peanut butter; add vanilla, salt, then gradually sugar and milk. Whip fluffy.
  6. Fill, stack, frost; chill 20 minutes.
  7. Pour steaming cream over chocolate; wait 5 minutes, stir. Cool until barely warm; spread atop cake. Chill 30 minutes.
