Chocolate peanut butter cake

Two 9-inch (23cm) layers. Quantities below make the whole cake: about 27% more than the two 8-inch version, keeping the same layer thickness. Measurements are rounded.

Adapted from Preppy Kitchen’s recipe. Flat-layer guide.

Cake

  • 203ml buttermilk
  • 127g beaten egg (beat 3 large eggs, then weigh without shells)
  • 152ml vegetable oil
  • 12.5ml vanilla (2½ tsp)
  • 84g unsweetened cocoa
  • 278g all-purpose flour
  • 464g sugar
  • 1¾ tsp baking soda (slightly scant)
  • 1¼ tsp baking powder
  • ⅝ tsp salt
  • 203ml hot coffee or water

Frosting

  • 287g softened unsalted butter
  • 274g creamy, no-stir peanut butter
  • 12.5ml vanilla (2½ tsp)
  • ⅛ tsp salt + a generous pinch
  • 506g powdered sugar
  • 51–63ml milk

Ganache

  • 143g chopped dark chocolate
  • 101ml heavy cream

Method

  1. Grease two 9-inch round cake pans, at least 2 inches deep; line bottoms with parchment. Heat oven to 350°F (175°C). Fill no more than ⅔ full; bake any extra batter separately.
  2. For flatter layers, soak fabric cake strips 5–10 minutes, squeeze, and fit around pans.
  3. Whisk dry cake ingredients. Combine buttermilk, eggs, oil, vanilla; stir into dry mixture, then add hot coffee.
  4. Divide evenly. Start checking at 30 minutes; bake until centers spring back and a tester has no wet batter. Timing may vary with your pan and cake strips. Cool 15 minutes; unmold and cool completely.
  5. Beat butter and peanut butter; add vanilla, salt, then gradually sugar and milk. Whip fluffy.
  6. Fill, stack, frost; chill 20 minutes.
  7. Pour steaming cream over chocolate; wait 5 minutes, stir. Cool until barely warm; spread atop cake. Chill 30 minutes.

Only one pan? Make two separate half-batches of the cake batter, mixing the second just before baking. Cool, clean, and re-line the pan between layers. Use half of every cake ingredient per batch, including 63.5g beaten egg. Make the frosting and ganache once using the full amounts above.
